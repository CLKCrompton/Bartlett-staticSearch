Timeline of the Editions

This page provides a detailed timeline of the publication history of Bartlett’s Familiar Quotations, spanning over 120 years.

1855 - First Edition

A Collection of Familiar Quotations, With Complete Indices of Authors and Subjects

295 pages. Published without author attribution. All 1000 copies sold out in three months.

1856 - Second Edition

A Collection of Familiar Quotations, Revised Edition

358 pages. Expanded content and refined structure.

1863 - Fourth Edition

Familiar Quotations: Chiefly from English Authors

480 pages. Significant emphasis on English literature. Included Montaigne’s epigraph.

1992 - Sixteenth Edition

Familiar Quotations: Modern and Ancient Sources

Edited by Justin Kaplan. Included significant updates and modern references.

2022 - Nineteenth Edition

The Latest Comprehensive Collection

Edited by Geoffrey O’Brien. Enhanced digital and print formats.
