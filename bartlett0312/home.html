Welcome to Bartlett’s Familiar Quotations Project

This project explores the evolution and composition of Bartlett's Familiar Quotations across its first ten editions, tracing what was considered “familiar” knowledge for an educated public over time. Through comprehensive data analysis and visualization, we uncover the systemic biases, editorial decisions, and historical contexts that shaped this iconic literary collection. From gender representation to geographic and occupational trends, the project highlights the voices that were included, those that were excluded, and the societal forces influencing these selections. By combining traditional research with digital tools, we aim to shed light on the construction of cultural canons and make this history accessible to all.

Explore the findings, visualizations, and insights to better understand how familiarity—and knowledge—was defined, curated, and perpetuated over time.
