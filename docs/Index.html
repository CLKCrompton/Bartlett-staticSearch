Timeline of the Editions

A chronological look at the various editions of Bartlett’s Familiar Quotations and significant events.

1855

First Edition: A Collection of Familiar Quotations, 528 pages by 169 authors.

1856

Second Edition: 358 pages.

1858

Third Edition: 446 pages.

1863

Fourth Edition: First Little, Brown Edition published.

1868

Fifth Edition

1871

Sixth Edition: An exact duplicate of the fifth edition.

1875

Seventh Edition

1882

Eighth Edition

1891

Ninth Edition: 1,158 pages.

1905

Bartlett’s Death: Bartlett passed away at the age of 84.

1914

Tenth Edition: Revised and Enlarged by Nathan Haskell Dole, 1,454 pages.

1937

Eleventh Edition: Edited by Christopher Morley and Louella D. Everett, 1,578 pages.

1948

Twelfth Edition: Edited by Christopher Morley and Louella D. Everett, 1,831 pages (6 times longer than the first edition).

1953

The Shorter Bartlett's Familiar Quotations: Based on the Twelfth Edition, edited by Kathleen Sproul, published by Permabooks of New York, 500 pages.

1955

Thirteenth Edition: Credited to the editors of Little, Brown. Special thanks to Morley, Everett, and Emily Morison Beck. Included recent materials and young authors like Bill Mauldin and Queen Elizabeth II.

1968

Fourteenth Edition: Edited by Emily Morison Beck.

1980

Fifteenth Edition: Edited by Emily Morison Beck, criticized for including trendy, youth-culture, and feminist quotes.

1993

Sixteenth Edition: Edited by Justin Kaplan, criticized for including pop culture.

2003

Seventeenth Edition: Edited by Justin Kaplan.

2012

Eighteenth Edition: Edited by Geoffrey O'Brien.

2021

Eighteenth Edition, Second Printing: Edited by Geoffrey O'Brien.
